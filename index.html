So what’s stopping it?

Every candidate needs
3 things to win.

Money

Campaigns cost millions. Whoever writes the biggest checks decides who can afford to run, and the money comes with strings.

Access

Party gatekeepers decide who gets on the ballot, who gets backed, and who gets told to wait their turn.

Visibility

Corporate media decides who gets seen and who counts as electable, all before anyone votes.

They don’t decide how you vote. They decide who your choices are.

There is a better way

Here’s what it looks like.

The patterns

Once you see these,
you can’t unsee them.

1

Conflict pays

+

News, campaigns and algorithms all make more money from a fight than from an agreement, so the things we agree on never get airtime.

Spot the conflict as the product, and it stops working on you.
2

Money makes the rules

+

Time with lawmakers is legal to buy. Whoever buys it gets results the rest of us don’t, and often gets to write the language too.

Follow the money, and you can see their next vote coming.
3

Party over policy

+

They run on what their voters want. Then they vote the way party leadership wants, because staying in line pays and breaking ranks costs.

Hold their vote up next to their promise.
4

The wrong boss

+

Disappoint a big donor and it costs you. Disappoint your voters and almost nothing happens.

Watch what they do all year, not just near election day.
5

Decided without you

+

The real choice often happens before you’re paying attention, in primaries and local meetings that are technically public and practically invisible.

Know where the real choice gets made.
↑ UpToward the people who fund them.
↓ DownToward the people who elected them.
